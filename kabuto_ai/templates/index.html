FAQ

Is KabutoAI GDPR-compliant?

We host data-sparingly, enable deletion & opt-out and advise on legal texts. Exact implementation depends on package & use case.

What info do you need to set up my bot?

We ask for a short description of your business, your goals (leads, support or sales), a handful of typical customer questions and answers and any special workflows or integrations you need. This allows us to train the bot in your tone and to set up custom flows.

Do you offer training and support?

Yes. Every package includes a quick-start guide. Our monthly plans include ongoing email or chat support, prompt optimisations and small updates depending on the tier.

Can I test the bot before committing?

Our live demo shows the core functionality. For a personalised pilot with your content we can set up a paid trial – the cost will be deducted from your package if you proceed.

Can the bot handle multiple languages?

Yes. Auto-detection (e.g., EN/DE) and answers in the right tone. More languages on request.

How fast is setup?

Starter in a few days, Pro/Enterprise depends on integrations. We provide clear step-by-step guides.

What are ongoing costs?

e.g., hosting (Render), Twilio (WhatsApp), possible API costs. We optimize for volume to keep ROI positive.

To tailor KabutoAI to your business, please provide the following information when you reach out. This ensures that we can prepare a precise offer and training materials for you.

  1. A short description of your company and services.
  2. Your goals: do you want to generate leads, offer support or sell products/services via chat?
  3. Tone and style: how should the bot speak (friendly, formal, humorous)?
  4. Examples of frequently asked questions and your preferred answers.
  5. Special requirements: integrations (CRM, booking tools), workflows or languages.
